MORSE cheatsheet

Firmware 0.7, every page and button. Learn on the device, key on its piano keys, read what you sent. MORSE on the web · try it in the browser · source and install · print

Anywhere

HOMEthe menu; on the menu, back where you were
ALGO knobthe next or previous page
FXsound on / off; off, FX lights and you read the key lights
K1hearing speed: characters at 5–40 WPM (20)
K2spacing (Farnsworth): letters and words pulled apart, 5 up to the hearing speed (8)
K3pitch 300–1200 Hz (600); what it receives sounds a major third higher
K4sending speed 5–40 WPM (10): the keyer, where KEY's decoder starts, RHYTHM's target
MASTERvolume

Turning a speed shows what it means (SEND 10 WPM DIT 120MS DAH 360MS). Settings save themselves 3 s after the last change. Not sure? SETTINGS > PRESET.

PRACTICE: flash cards

PLAYstart a round; after the prompt, hear it again
SELECTon the start page: a round, or TEST (a placement test: the next two groups once each, what you get right can be marked known)
E4 F4 G4 A4pick one of four: the four lit keys are the choices on the screen, left to right
SELECT + SELor turn to a choice (it blinks) and press SEL
piano keyscards that ask you to key it: when your turn shows, key with the keyer you set; judged by your own rhythm (a dah about three dits), any speed
SELon a keyed card: don't know (counted wrong, the answer shows)
any piano keya new letter: next card once seen and heard; an answer: go on without waiting
SEQlearning by sound: a peek at the code of a new letter or an answer (hidden again on the next card; never on hear-and-choose)
HOMEleave; what you answered is kept

A keyed card's result: RIGHT / ALMOST / NO, a grade, READ AS (what the decoder read), and SHOULD (the standard rhythm) over YOU (what you pressed), the mark that flipped in red. ALMOST neither moves the card up nor back to the start; it comes again at the end of the round. NEXT GROUP WAITS ON: the letters holding back the next group.

LISTEN: endless practice

PLAYstart / stop
SELECTprogram: KOCH GROUPS, WORDS, QSO, ABBREVIATIONS, SAYINGS
SEQshow / hide the text being played (hidden, a line shows once it has played)
SELhidden: a peek at this line

KEY: send, it reads you

piano keysstraight key: any key. Iambic: the left half (F3–F4) dit, the right half (F♯4–G5) dah; SETTINGS > PADDLES swaps them
SEQview: TEXT → CODE (your dits and dahs under each letter) → TIMING (every mark drawn to length)
OCT-clear the screen

White is what you sent, amber what came in (MIDI, or text from the computer). The top line: the speed the decoder hears, the dah:dit ratio (3.0 is standard) and the gap inside a letter (1.0); amber when far off. TIMING: the grey bars are the target dit and dah, a red line a pause long enough to split a letter in two. With a computer connected, the keying goes out over USB-MIDI or Bluetooth MIDI in the Vail format.

RHYTHM: short and long

piano keyskey to the target
SELECTdrill: DITS, DAHS, DIT DAH, SOS, FREE
PLAYstart again (the counts cleared)
K4the target speed

The frame is the target length, the bar what you pressed: green good, yellow off, red the other kind. The tip below looks at the last 16 marks.

SELLIVE (each key plays and goes out) / COMPOSE (silent until PLAY sends the line)

SETTINGS > TYPE KEYS changes the layout: A–Z left to right, or Koch order. A phrase's meaning shows on the screen.

Questions that change your progress (SAVE CHANGES?, MARK THESE KNOWN?, LOAD THIS BACKUP?) take only PLAY (yes) and HOME (no).

With a computer

MORSE on the web (Chrome or Edge, nothing to install): see what the FM-1 reads, send it text to play, back up and restore your progress and, from 0.7, your settings. Over USB, or Bluetooth MIDI (0.5+: on a Mac, Audio MIDI Setup → Show MIDI Studio → Bluetooth → Connect FM-1 XXXX, again after the FM-1 restarts), or the page's own Over Bluetooth, directly. Vail takes the keying as it is. The Python host (morse type, morse send, morse backup / restore) is in M-VAVE_FM-1.
