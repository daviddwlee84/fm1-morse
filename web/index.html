MORSE on the web

See what your FM-1 reads, send it text to copy, and back up what it has learnt in MORSE. Chrome or Edge on a computer, the FM-1 running MORSE (backups: 0.4 or later) on USB, or on Bluetooth with 0.5: on a Mac, connect FM-1 XXXX first in Audio MIDI Setup (Window, Show MIDI Studio, the Bluetooth button). Nothing to install.

1. Connect

not connected

The browser asks to use MIDI devices with system exclusive messages: allow it.

2. Live

What the FM-1 reads: key on its KEY page, or type on its TYPE page.

Send text: the FM-1 plays it as received (amber on its KEY page), at its hearing speed. <SK> runs letters together as one prosign.

3. Back up

Nothing changes on the FM-1. The file is plain JSON: every letter's two boxes (0..5), the rounds.

4. Restore

The FM-1 shows what it got and asks: press PLAY on it to load, HOME to keep what is there.
