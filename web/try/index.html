Try MORSE

MORSE on an FM-1 in your browser: the same firmware you would install, run by the fm1-emu emulator. Press Start on the screen, then click the synth once so it hears your keyboard. Back to MORSE on the web · cheatsheet · source.

Things to try

  1. Send. It starts on the menu. Turn SELECT to KEY, press SEL, and key on the piano keys: short presses are dits, long ones dahs. It writes what it read.
  2. Hear. HOME, then LISTEN, PLAY: endless practice at 20 WPM with the gaps stretched (Farnsworth). SELECT changes the program: Koch groups, words, a QSO.
  3. Learn. PRACTICE, PLAY: flash cards by sound, five letters first (K M U R E).
  4. Type. TYPE: every key is a letter, digit or CW phrase; it plays them.
  5. Set up. SETTINGS, the last row: START PAGE, the page it starts on.

Keys on the computer

  • The piano keys: A S D F G H … are white keys from C4, W E T … the black ones. Any of them is a straight key.
  • Buttons and knobs: click or drag them on the panel.
  • The emulator's own guide has the whole map and MIDI in.

Here, not on an FM-1

  • No Bluetooth, and no MIDI out to Vail: on a real FM-1 the keying goes out over USB-MIDI or Bluetooth MIDI.
  • What it learns is kept in this browser, not on a device. Back-ups of a real FM-1's progress are on MORSE on the web.
  • Timing follows your computer: a slow one makes the keying less even than the real thing.
